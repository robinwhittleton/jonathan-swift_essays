## Selection of subjects

This collection intentionally omits:

- Prayers
- Sermons
- Most letters
- Political reports of the day
- Political pamphlets
- Poems
- Memoirs
- Biographies
- Most newspaper op-eds

See the spreadsheet at https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1BJg89IX-ipE3rNqmLTFNVAJ9z5_BdfBtuDsv632HC3U/ for collection data.

## Italics

The source transcriptions are from a later date than the original scans: the main differences are the removal of the long-s (ſ) and much of the original’s italicisation. In my opinion the production reads better for these changes, so they’ve been retained. (Some removal of sexual material, e.g. reference to “whores”, was also noted during the production process: where found this has been reinstated.)
